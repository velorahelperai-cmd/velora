## Native invariants and legacy bridge

1. Every read/write validates genuine current native actor/broker lineage, app footprint and active owner before revealing company/branch data. Reuse B1/B2 immutable paid order/slot/right/selected-channel checks. A company in setup is permitted only through those paid facts; caller paid flags, labels or persona are not authority.
2. Persist immutable account/company/branch/right/channel-selection/flow/native-session/legacy-attempt binding, server generation and expiry before provider work. Pin selected entitlement/revision at start; revalidate at claim, stage, finalization and recovery. ClientOpId binds the exact payload and branch; retry returns same committed receipt, cross-branch/changed-payload reuse denies. A native lock/unique constraint enforces competing attempts/asset claims, not JS prechecks.
3. Associate the legacy attempt and new scope in one database transaction. Store adapters use branch-aware native wrappers for claim, Vault stage/checkpoint, subscription claim and finalization; their transactions recheck paid scope and exact attempt. Atomically persist the branch-link reference with legacy finalization. A precheck followed by an unbound legacy finalize is not acceptable. Preserve old endpoints/metadata unless a specifically reviewed additive bridge needs an explicitly identified change; do not rewrite installed migrations.
4. Old company links and active/unknown legacy attempts without matching immutable B3 provenance cause `legacy_ambiguous`/reconciliation denial, preserved unchanged. A new branch never adopts those records. Existing company-wide uniqueness/capacity remains a reported legacy constraint; do not silently introduce a product policy limiting paid branches or remove uniqueness to enable unsafe sharing. If supporting multiple branches requires a routing/asset-sharing policy beyond existing contracts, identify the exact gap and continue independent safe invariants/tests. Fresh read-only native catalog proof `task/es02-b3-legacy-constraints.json` confirms WA `UNIQUE(company_id)`, Coexistence `PRIMARY KEY(company_id)`, and Instagram `instagram_one_active_company` unique(company_id) WHERE status='active', plus active-account uniqueness. These existing constraints are infrastructure limits, not an approved one-branch product policy.
5. Connected projection joins exact bound branch provenance to the same authoritative native provider link and generation, verified authorization/expiry/revocation/account/app/contract evidence, and required subscription/Coexistence live-verification facts. Missing/stale/mismatched evidence is unavailable/reconciliation, never verified. Do not equate completed OAuth, an active-looking company row, selected channels or a successful launch callback with connected proof. Read is DB-only; `loadInstagramOwnerConnection` profile fetch and Coexistence reconciliation are not read adapters for this contract.
6. Preserve single-dispatch and UNKNOWN behavior. Ambiguous provider response/commit keeps claimed/staged/unknown recovery state; no automatic replay, expiry reset or new attempt that authorizes another exchange. Recovery reads exact committed records; subscription/provider effects occur only through their existing explicit claimed protocol.
7. No channel readiness, billing/credits/clock reconciliation, consent-policy expansion, history sync opt-in, AI replies or launch writes. Connection verification is only a channel fact, not overall assistant readiness.
